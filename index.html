Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2026

LeadSathi AI aapki privacy ka samman karta hai. Ye policy batati hai ki hum aapki jaankari WhatsApp automation service ke through kaise collect aur use karte hain.

1. Hum Kya Collect Karte Hain

  • Aapka naam aur WhatsApp number
  • Aapki requirement/budget se judi jaankari jo aap chat mein share karte hain
  • Appointment/meeting preferences

2. Use Kaise Hota Hai

  • Aapke sawaalon ka reply dene ke liye
  • Aapki requirement samajh kar relevant jaankari bhejne ke liye
  • Follow-up aur appointment schedule karne ke liye

3. Data Kahan Store Hota Hai

Aapki jaankari secure tarike se store hoti hai. Hum aapka data kisi third-party ko nahi bechte ya share nahi karte.

4. WhatsApp/Meta

Hamara automation Meta ke official WhatsApp Business Platform par chalta hai, isliye Meta ki apni privacy policy bhi apply hoti hai: whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy
